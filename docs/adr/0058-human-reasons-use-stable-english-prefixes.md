# Human reasons use stable English prefixes

Human-gate reasons use fixed English prefixes such as `HUMAN_REVIEW_PENDING`, `HUMAN_REVIEW_RECORDED`, `HUMAN_REVIEW_REJECTED`, `HUMAN_APPROVAL_STALE`, and `HUMAN_REVIEW_UNCHANGED`. The workflow request and rejection feedback may remain in Chinese or any other language. The prefix gives skills and tests a stable machine-readable marker while the rest of the single-line reason remains human-readable.
